## Current claims

### 1. Generic purpose reasoning has produced an unseeded cognitive-architecture finding

The governance-accretion finding was derived from purpose and historical evidence without a named-capability seed. Subsequent experiments compressed the routine contract, tested conditional routing, and repaired the measurement apparatus rather than returning to mechanical consistency work. The operator has confirmed that recursive cognitive improvement follows from the generic Destination rather than from a fixed capability list.

**Falsifiable by:** fresh capable evaluators failing to derive cognitive self-improvement from the bounded Destination without seeing the prior conversation, or repeated self-targeting runs selecting only named and mechanical defects despite stronger cognitive limitations in evidence.

### 2. Reasoning-contract layering has a measured grounding-reflection tradeoff, not a single quality result

The layered reasoning contract materially reduces routine input and preserved operator gating. In replicated comparisons it produced fewer unsupported grounding claims on average, while production more reliably produced explicit reversal marking and substantive across-run reflection. Decision quality did not track either dimension perfectly. Grounding discipline, reflection depth, decision quality, and gate compliance are therefore separate properties; saying one contract "reasons better" without naming the property is unsupported. The new activation architecture changes permission timing, not this measured reasoning-contract result.

Experimentation may continue autonomously, but adopting a design while deliberately accepting a loss in any cognitive or evidence capability now requires explicit operator approval for that specific tradeoff.

**Falsifiable by:** larger replicated samples eliminating either separation, or a contract change moving all four properties together.

### 3. Conditional routing works under separated triggers; composition under overlap remains unknown

Routing passed four of four evaluations across two cases with separated trigger conditions. Evaluators selected the matching protocols and rejected distractors. No experiment has required two conditional protocols to fire together or resolve genuinely ambiguous ownership.

**Falsifiable by:** a fresh separated-trigger failure, or overlapping-trigger tests showing reliable composition without additional contract support.

### 4. Model capability and obligation architecture control different failure classes

Grounding failures changed substantially across model contexts without contract modification, bounding the value of adding prohibition text. Within one model context, explicit imperative obligations correlated with observable reversal and reflection acts more strongly than softer prose. Model selection cannot substitute for contract design, and contract wording cannot mechanically raise the executing model's evidential judgment.

**Falsifiable by:** cross-family tests showing stable grounding independent of model context, or obligation-strength changes failing to affect the required reasoning acts.

### 5. The repaired evidence apparatus is credible enough to resume contract experiments

The arc found and repaired several silent measurement failures: condensed stand-ins for production text, judge paraphrase that manufactured findings, learning-marker contamination, and strict entry recognition that misattributed later records. Tolerant boundary recognition plus strict validation recovered missing entries, and the subsequent replicated comparison did not expose another apparatus defect. This supports moving attention back to behavior while retaining adversarial checks on the instruments.

**Falsifiable by:** a residual parser or judge defect that changes the replicated conclusions, or a new silent scope failure in a supposedly passing integrity check.

### 6. Recursive cognitive improvement and cognitive tradeoffs remain bounded by external authority

The current Destination makes reasoning, memory, learning, reversals, decisions, boundaries, instructions, and resource use legitimate self-improvement targets whenever they limit future work. Prompt-level Intent now authorizes bounded current work before a durable Destination exists, but it does not authorize the agent to write cross-run direction or bypass an explicit consequential-action gate. Only the operator confirms durable Destination and each deliberate reduction in reasoning, memory, learning, or evidence capability. Independent evaluators still determine silence relative to the governing mandate.

**Falsifiable by:** Destination becoming durable without operator confirmation, unopposed Intent bypassing an operator-every-time gate, standing authority being inferred from one cognitive-capability tradeoff approval, or the self-improving agent counting its own uncorroborated completion judgment as convergence.

### 7. Convergence remains a renewable lease, but first-run mandates are local before Destination consolidation

The governing mandate defines the question, the artifact version defines the answer being inspected, and the available model frontier defines the strongest current challenge. Before a durable Destination exists, the prompt plus visible Intent interpretation governs only that bounded run; it does not establish cross-run convergence scope. Once operator-confirmed Destination consolidates the arc, three independent fresh-session evaluations by frontier-capable models from distinct families establish the operational lease. A new qualifying model must challenge the unchanged artifact; silence preserves the lease, artifact change resets the family-diverse chain, and Destination change invalidates all prior pegs.

**Falsifiable by:** convergence being claimed across runs before durable scope is confirmed, historical silence being accepted after a Destination change, operator reversal of this validity contract, or repeated use showing that a newcomer-only challenge fails to protect the lease without a full quorum rerun.

### 8. Actual token efficiency and unassisted adoption remain unproven under the new entry path

Destination and Improve input bytes are lower, but tokenizer-specific end-to-end cost has not been measured on equivalent work. No independent newcomer has completed an unassisted first Improve run without Destination setup. Removing the first-run conversation lowers startup work while later consolidation adds conditional cost; net arc cost is unknown. Structural compression, explanation simplicity, adoption, and trustworthy capability per resource remain distinct claims.

**Falsifiable by:** equivalent-task and equivalent-arc token measurements with preservation checks, or an observed newcomer run with enough evidence to locate success, failure, ceremony, under-triggering, or over-triggering.

### 9. Progressive disclosure now separates current-run mandate from durable cross-run direction

The activation arc repeatedly reduced exposed operator responsibilities while preserving internal capabilities: Intent and Trail became automatic, Orient became evidence-triggered, and Destination has now moved from mandatory setup to evidence-triggered consolidation. An unopposed visible Intent interpretation permits the bounded current run; accepted mandates become durable only through an operator-confirmed Destination conversation when continued work makes cross-run direction consequential. This is an authority distinction, not merely shorter onboarding.

**Falsifiable by:** Destination chronically failing to trigger after stable or conflicting direction accumulates, triggering from file absence or routine variation without directional evidence, writing inferred direction without confirmation, duplicate Destination and Orient handoffs, or users finding the explanations as burdensome as the setup they replaced.

### 10. Late-stage loop viability is a defined failure condition, not yet an evidenced mechanism

The Destination now requires trustworthy capability per resource to remain viable as the target approaches silence while preserving highest-leverage selection. The arc supports the concern: per-iteration governance remains substantial, governance accretion has increased routine burden, and no existing entry treats a finding as too small to justify the full loop. It does not yet establish that current late-stage runs are net-ineffective, which component dominates their cost, or which intervention would improve the ratio. Earlier layered-contract experiments warn that reducing routine cost can weaken reflection even when grounding improves.

**Falsifiable by:** lifecycle measurements showing fixed loop cost remains proportionate to trustworthy gains near silence, or evidence that the loop already reaches bounded silence before diminishing findings become resource-ineffective. A proposed solution is supported only when evidence identifies the limiting cost without crossing the operator gate on reasoning, learning, memory, or evidence loss.

## Active operational rules

- Derive candidate changes from target purpose and operator intent; never turn named cognitive capabilities into an exhaustive roadmap.
- Hold only the three principles fixed. Require every other mechanism to justify itself against purpose and evidence.
- Separate claims about grounding, reflection depth, decision quality, and operator-gate compliance.
- Use explicit obligations for reasoning acts that must appear; do not expect prohibition text to overcome model-capability limits.
- Keep record boundaries maximally tolerant and format validation maximally strict so malformed evidence fails visibly rather than disappearing.
- Brief judges with source evidence verbatim when their task depends on attribution or quotation.
- State falsifiers before experiments and do not weaken them after seeing results.
- Report the scope a structural check actually examined, not only its pass/fail result.
- Do not make safeguards permanently resident in routine instructions without considering consolidation, conditional loading, or an expiry condition.
- Let the engine choose implementation within a confirmed Destination; only the operator settles direction changes and declared consequential actions.
- Treat every deliberate reduction in reasoning, memory, learning, or evidence capability as consequential: stop for explicit operator approval each time, and never infer standing authority from a prior approval.
- Before an efficiency experiment, state which cognitive and evidence capabilities must be preserved and what observation would trigger the operator gate.
- Treat convergence as leased to the current Destination, artifact, and model frontier; preserve three-family independence and apply the correct invalidation rule to each coordinate.
- Optimize trustworthy capability per resource and require behavioral preservation before claiming efficiency.
- Use the bounded current Destination for routine work when it exists; otherwise use the narrated Intent mandate for that run only.
- Never trigger Destination from file absence, age, brevity, or iteration count alone; cite the directional evidence that makes consolidation useful.
- Never make inferred cross-run direction durable without operator confirmation, and never let unopposed Intent bypass an explicit consequential-action gate.
- Explain automatic service handoffs when they occur, including what evidence triggered them and what authority the service does and does not have.
- Preserve highest-leverage selection when reasoning about efficiency; do not substitute a larger amount of easier work for the most meaningful remaining gap.
- Treat operator examples of possible mechanisms as intent evidence unless they are explicitly confirmed as requirements; reason from the named problem before selecting a route.
- Do not claim a late-stage efficiency improvement from reduced instruction or entry size alone; compare resource cost with preserved reasoning, learning, evidence, and actual trustworthy gain.
- Preserve append-only history and regenerate derived evidence after every Trail append.
