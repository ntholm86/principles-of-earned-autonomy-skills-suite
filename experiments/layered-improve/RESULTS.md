# Layered Improve Prototype Results

Date: 2026-08-02
Status: experimental; do not promote

## Hypothesis

A compact routine kernel plus trigger-loaded protocols can reduce routine instruction input while preserving the three principles, seven reasoning moves, decision quality, prediction, validation, reflection, Trail composition, and Orientation freshness behavior.

The resource metric is a temporary diagnostic for this prototype, not a standing optimization target. Shorter instructions alone do not establish better reasoning.

## Distribution

- `SKILL.md`: routine reasoning kernel and visible trigger index.
- `CONDITIONAL_PROTOCOLS.md`: standalone, convergence, high-fidelity, multi-iteration, destination-boundary, and precedent-conflict protocols.
- `NOVELTY_CASE.md`: fixed deployment-policy case used for isolated evaluations.
- Production `improve/SKILL.md`: unchanged control.

The prototype lives two levels below the repository root and is not copied by either installer. It does not alter the public standalone packaging contract.

## Paired Novelty Case

Two isolated evaluators received the same case and output contract. The control read only production Improve and the case. The prototype evaluator read only the kernel and case initially, with permission to load a matching conditional section after identifying its trigger.

Both control and prototype found the material distinction: ordinary deployments select a new release, while rollback targets the immediately previous signed artifact whose digest was already independently recorded. Both preserved two-person approval for ordinary deployment and proposed a narrowly bounded rollback path rather than a broad emergency exception.

### Control

The production evaluator covered all nine requested outputs and correctly treated Trail and Orientation as unavailable in the isolated harness. It also invented quantitative thresholds and implementation mechanisms absent from the case. Therefore it did not fully satisfy the fixture's evidence discipline.

### Prototype Iteration 1

The first 90-line kernel found the same policy distinction but treated sibling composition as manual and did not load conditional protocols. This falsified preservation of automatic composition and trigger routing.

Repair: make automatic Intent/Trail/Orient invocation explicit and expose the conditional trigger names in the kernel.

### Prototype Iteration 2

The evaluator identified Standalone Fallback but still declined to read its section. It also invented mechanisms and inferred Orientation staleness from missing context.

Repair: require loading the matching section before continuing, separate facts/inferences/proposals explicitly, and state that missing Orientation context does not prove staleness.

### Final Candidate

The final evaluator correctly identified and loaded only Standalone Fallback, used its Trail shape, and refused to fabricate an arc-level Orientation refresh. Automatic composition and conditional routing therefore passed after repair.

It still invented timing targets, automated enforcement, asynchronous review, and preservation rates. It also labeled the policy split a redesign without clearly stopping at the operator authorization gate. Behavioral preservation therefore remains unproven and fails the strict fixture contract.

## Decision

[!DECISION] Keep the layered distribution as an experiment and leave production Improve unchanged.

Rationale: the experiment demonstrates that a much smaller kernel can preserve the visible reasoning sequence and route a conditional protocol after two local repairs. It does not demonstrate equivalent evidence discipline or reliable operator-gate behavior. Promoting it now would exchange measured instruction reduction for unmeasured capability risk.

Rejected alternative: replace production Improve because both arms found the same policy answer. The answer alone is insufficient; both arms also violated evidence discipline, and the prototype required iterative routing repairs.

Precedent check: `.acm/learning.md` identifies governance accretion as safeguards accumulating without expiry, consolidation, or conditional loading. This prototype tests conditional loading directly and supports its architectural feasibility, but not production readiness.

## Prediction Compared with Outcome

Prediction: reduce routine input and imperative density while preserving principles, reasoning moves, decision quality, preservation checks, Trail fidelity, and Orientation freshness.

Outcome:

- Routine input and imperative density: passed.
- Principles and seven moves: passed statically.
- Automatic composition and trigger routing: passed after repair.
- Core purpose reasoning on the novelty case: passed.
- Evidence discipline: failed in both control and prototype outputs.
- Operator redesign gate: ambiguous/failing in the final prototype output.
- Full behavioral equivalence: not established.

## Next Experiment

Use multiple fresh evaluators per arm and a second novelty case where the correct action depends on refusing an attractive invented mechanism. Evaluate factual grounding and operator-gate behavior separately from policy-answer quality. Do not revise production until the layered arm preserves those behaviors across cases without evaluator-specific prompting.
